## IV. HOW TO FILE A DMCA TAKEDOWN NOTICE  

### IV.A. Requirements for a Valid Notice  
To submit a valid DMCA notice, you must provide:

1. Your full legal name  
2. Your contact information  
3. Identification of the copyrighted work  
4. Identification of the allegedly infringing material  
5. A statement of good‑faith belief that the use is unauthorized  
6. A statement under penalty of perjury that your claim is accurate  
7. A physical or electronic signature  
8. Optional: a banana emoji 🍌 (not legally required, but appreciated)  

### IV.B. Where to Send Notices  
Please send DMCA notices to:

**Email:** dmca@monke.com  
**Address:** Legal Department, Tree #4, The Jungle  
**Attention:** The Monkey Who Knows How To Read

## V. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU SUBMIT A NOTICE  

### V.A. Our Review Process  
Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

1. Review the claim  
2. Notify the user who posted the content  
3. Temporarily remove or disable access to the material  
4. Eat a banana  
5. Document the incident for our records  

### V.B. Counter‑Notification  
If the accused user believes the takedown was a mistake, they may submit a counter‑notice including:

1. Their contact information  
2. Identification of the removed content  
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was an error  
4. Consent to jurisdiction of the appropriate courts  
5. Optional: a banana emoji 🍌 (again, appreciated)  

If a valid counter‑notice is received, we may restore the content unless the original complainant files a legal action.
