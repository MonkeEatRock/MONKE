## II. WHAT ARE COOKIES  
Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They serve various purposes, such as enabling website functionality, improving performance, and occasionally distracting the server monkeys with shiny objects.

Cookies may include, but are not limited to:

1. **Session Cookies** – Temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser  
2. **Persistent Cookies** – Remain on your device until manually removed  
3. **Third‑Party Cookies** – Placed by external services (rare, but possible)  
4. **Mystery Cookies** – We don’t know where they came from, but they’re harmless
